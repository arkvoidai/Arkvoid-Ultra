# ARKVOID Creature Art Bible

Tags: `[V]` verified this session, `[K]` prior knowledge, `[D]` design decision. **No creature model exists.** Everything below is a concept brief.

## 1. Method `[D]`
1. Start from a real animal anatomy family (ungulate, mustelid, corvid, amphibian, arachnid, bovid).
2. Make **one** structural alteration with a stated survival reason (a resonance organ, an anchoring claw, a sound-sensing frill).
3. Place it in the ecology: niche, food, predator, habitat zone (ENVIRONMENT zones Z0–Z4).
4. Define behaviour tells before the model: what it does when calm, alert, hunting, hurt.
5. Silhouette test: readable as a flat black shape at 40 m.
6. Avoid stock mythological shapes (dragon, phoenix, qilin, nine-tailed fox) as base designs.

## 2. Resonance in creatures
Creatures show resonance physically: tuned horn spirals, hollow bone chambers, throat sacs, skin that ripples with standing waves. They do not glow by default. Boss-tier creatures may reach R3–R4 effects under the emission caps.

## 4. Production standards
| Tier | Triangles LOD0 | Joints | Texture | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard creature | ≤ 25,000 | ≤ 60 | 2K albedo/normal/ORM | 3 LODs |
| Major | ≤ 45,000 | ≤ 90 | 2K plus a 1K detail | Fur cards or scale shells |
| Boss | ≤ 60,000 | ≤ 120 | 2K × 2 sets | Cinematic LOD+ allowed |
Locomotion must be anatomically correct (gait cycles from reference). Mass drives animation timing: heavier creatures have longer anticipation and recovery. Boss presentation: a readable silhouette on approach, a unique arena logic tied to terrain (e.g., scree that rings).

## 5. Rules
- Eyes must always show intent (a predator's eyes face forward, prey's lateral).
- No creature without a diet.
- Ecological scaling: apex creatures ≤ 1 per 4 km² of their zone.
- Creature palettes obey the world palette; accent colours only on display organs.

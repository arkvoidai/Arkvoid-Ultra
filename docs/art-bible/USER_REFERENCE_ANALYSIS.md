# User Reference Analysis (U01–U10)

Date: 2026-11-18. Scope: the ten images supplied with Prompt 03. Fields follow the Prompt 03 list (1 identity … 15 avoid). Everything below is read from the images themselves; nothing is assumed about their authorship.

## Global findings (read first)
1. **All ten appear AI-generated.** Tells seen across the set: structures with no load path, repeated tiny pagodas/spires used as texture, hair fusing into cloth, ambiguous jewellery attachment, mirrored/inconsistent reflections, unreadable pseudo-glyphs on banners and steles, physically incompatible sky events (aurora with a low sun, planets in a daytime sky). U05 is a phone screenshot of an image viewer (status bar, Edit/Markup/Resize buttons). I cannot verify the generator or its terms. They are therefore **Category D (generated concept references) or inspiration-only**. They never ship and are never traced or painted over for production art.
2. **The set shares one fingerprint**: golden-hour sun, sea of cloud, waterfalls everywhere, small pagodas, gold ornament, glow, floating or impossible architecture, planets. That is exactly the overuse list in Prompt 03 §7. ARKVOID keeps the images' *composition and depth lessons* and rejects their *surface vocabulary*.
3. What is genuinely strong in the set is **image craft**, not world design: layered depth, value hierarchy, a single accent hue, a small human for scale, a clear leading line. Those are retained as principles (RP-xx) and mapped in the matrix at the end.
4. Direction conflict to resolve: U01/U02 show non-human features (pointed ears, wings) and non-functional costume. The brief asks for realistic anatomy and functional clothing, so those features are rejected (RJ-08).

## U01 — `character-02.webp` (1024×1536, portrait)
1. **Identity:** Female character in a space-facing palace atrium. Saved copy `0adcc539-image.webp`, sha256 `b655687c696841e5…`.
2. **Subject:** Long-haired woman, three-quarter turn, hand on a crystal railing; ringed planet and floating tiered platforms behind.
3. **Composition:** Figure fills the left two-thirds; staircase ribbon sweeps from left-mid to right; planet is a large soft disc behind the head.
4. **Camera:** Low angle looking up, moderate-wide lens; makes the figure monumental.
5. **Hierarchy:** Face (brightest, sharpest) → headdress → sheer sleeve with gold filigree → planet → tiny figures on stairs.
6. **Scale:** Tiny uniformed people at left give a very large atrium; the platforms on the right have no scale reference.
7. **Lighting:** Warm key from camera-left on the face; cool blue-violet rim/backlight from space; scattered sparkle highlights.
8. **Colour:** Cream/white and crimson on blue-violet; warm figure against cool ground (complementary), gold as accent. Face and hands carry the highest value.
9. **Architecture:** Baroque/Beaux-Arts arches, chandeliers used as platforms, crystal balustrade. Not East Asian, not mountain.
10. **Materials:** Sheer organza with metallic filigree, gold wire jewellery with hanging gems, crystal, polished marble.
11. **Character:** Pointed ears, violet irises, dark hair graded to silver, phoenix-wing crown with chains, off-shoulder layered robe (sheer over opaque red). Pose reads regal.
12. **Density:** High everywhere; background density is mostly repeated small forms.
13. **Depth:** Strong. Foreground sharp, mid soft, far cool and low contrast; value drops with distance.
14. **Retain:** Low-angle hero framing for authority beats (RP-08); sheer-over-opaque garment layering as a cloth-sim strategy (RP-09); warm key / cool rim split (RP-02); crown silhouette that reads at distance.
15. **Avoid / AI errors:** Headdress chains and earrings do not visibly attach; hair strands merge with fabric; platforms hang on ornamental stems that connect to nothing (RJ-01); crystal railing refractions inconsistent; costume has no seams, closures or support (RJ-08); ears and exaggerated proportions conflict with realistic anatomy; Baroque vocabulary (RJ-07); planet-through-glass cliché (RJ-06).
